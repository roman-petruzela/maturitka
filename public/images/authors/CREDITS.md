All authors listed here died more than 70 years ago (mostly well over 100),
so every image is in the public domain in the EU/US — no attribution is
legally required, but source is noted here for traceability.

The Jan Vilímek portraits (1860–1938) are from his "České album" series of
the 1880s–90s, published in period magazines (Humoristické listy, Zlatá
Praha, Světozor); Vilímek died in 1938, so pma-70 is long past.
