The specific part of maturita topic 15 (Services and accommodation), at B2 level: buying and selling online, and how a typical company is organised.

## E-commerce

**E-commerce** (electronic commerce) means buying and selling goods and services over the internet – in **online shops** (e-shops), on **marketplaces** such as Amazon or Allegro, through apps or social networks.

### Types of e-commerce

| Type | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **B2C** (business to consumer) | a company sells to customers | an online electronics shop |
| **B2B** (business to business) | companies trade with each other | a firm ordering office supplies |
| **C2C** (consumer to consumer) | people sell to each other | second-hand clothes apps, online bazaars |
| **C2B** | individuals sell to companies | freelancers, influencers |

### How an online purchase works

1. The customer **browses** the **product catalogue**, uses **search** and **filters** and reads **reviews**.
2. He or she puts the product into the **shopping cart** (AmE) / **basket** (BrE).
3. At the **checkout** the customer logs in or orders as a guest, chooses **delivery** (courier, pick-up point, parcel locker) and **payment** (card, online banking, digital wallet such as Apple Pay, cash on delivery, "buy now, pay later").
4. The payment goes through a secure **payment gateway**; the website uses **HTTPS** to encrypt the data.
5. The customer gets an **order confirmation** by email, can **track the parcel** and, in the EU, may **return** the goods within **14 days** without giving a reason.

### What a good e-shop needs

A clear and fast website that works on mobile phones (**responsive design**), good photos and descriptions, simple checkout, several payment and delivery options, **customer support** (chat, email, phone), security and **GDPR** compliance, and **online marketing** – search engine optimisation (**SEO**), ads, newsletters and social media.

### Advantages and disadvantages

**For customers:** shopping 24/7 from home, a wide choice, easy price comparison and often lower prices – but you can't touch or try the goods, you have to wait for delivery, and there is a risk of **fraud** or fake shops.

**For companies:** lower costs than a real shop, customers from all over the world, data about customer behaviour – but strong competition, the cost of delivery and returns, and the need to protect the site from cyber attacks.

## Parts of a company

### Types of companies

In English-speaking countries you will meet a **sole trader** (self-employed person), a **partnership**, a **private limited company** (Ltd – similar to the Czech s.r.o.) and a **public limited company** (plc, or a corporation – Inc. – in the USA, similar to a.s.), whose **shares** can be traded on the stock exchange.

### Structure

A company is usually organised into **departments**, shown in an **organisational chart**:

| Department | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| **management / board of directors** | strategy and important decisions; led by the **CEO** (chief executive officer) |
| **finance / accounting** | money, budgets, invoices, taxes, salaries |
| **human resources (HR)** | hiring new employees, training, contracts, working conditions |
| **production / operations** | making the products or providing the services |
| **research and development (R&D)** | new products and improvements |
| **sales** | selling to customers, contracts, prices |
| **marketing** | market research, advertising, the brand, social media |
| **purchasing / logistics** | buying materials, the warehouse, transport |
| **customer service** | help for customers, complaints, returns |
| **IT department** | computers, networks, software, security and support for the other departments |
| **legal department** | contracts and legal issues |

Other important terms: **headquarters** (the main office), **branch** (a local office), **employees** and their **line manager**, **turnover** (the total amount of money from sales) and **profit** (what is left after paying the costs).

## Useful phrases

- *The customer adds… to the basket and then…*
- *The main advantage of shopping online is…, whereas…*
- *The… department is responsible for…*
- *If I ran an online shop, I would…*
- *I'd rather buy… in a real shop, because…*
