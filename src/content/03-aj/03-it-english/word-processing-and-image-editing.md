The specific part of maturita topic 20 (Science and technology), at B2 level: what word processors and image editors are used for and the most important terms you need to describe working with them.

## Word processing

A **word processor** is a program for writing, editing and formatting text documents. The best known are **Microsoft Word**, **Google Docs**, **LibreOffice Writer** and Apple **Pages**. People use them for letters, CVs, reports, school essays, contracts and books.

### What you can do in a word processor

- **Typing and editing** – insert, delete, **copy** (Ctrl+C), **cut** (Ctrl+X), **paste** (Ctrl+V), **undo** (Ctrl+Z), **find and replace**.
- **Character formatting** – the **font** (typeface), **font size**, **bold**, *italics*, underlining, colour, **superscript** and **subscript**.
- **Paragraph formatting** – **alignment** (left, centred, right, **justified**), **line spacing**, **indentation**, **bulleted** and **numbered lists**.
- **Styles** – predefined formats for headings and body text. Using styles keeps the document consistent and lets the program create an automatic **table of contents**.
- **Page layout** – page size and **orientation** (portrait / landscape), **margins**, **columns**, **page breaks**, **headers and footers**, **page numbers**.
- **Inserting objects** – tables, pictures, charts, shapes, hyperlinks, **footnotes**, equations.
- **Tools** – **spell checker** and grammar checker, word count, **track changes** and **comments** for working with others, **mail merge** for sending one letter to many people.
- **Saving and exporting** – .docx, .odt, or **PDF** for sharing a final version that looks the same on every device.

### Tips for a well-formatted document

Use styles instead of formatting every heading by hand; never use spaces or empty lines to move text – use indentation, tabs and page breaks; keep one or two fonts; and number pictures and tables with **captions**.

## Image editing

An **image editor** is a program for creating and changing digital pictures. Professionals use **Adobe Photoshop** (raster graphics) and **Adobe Illustrator** (vector graphics); free alternatives are **GIMP**, **Inkscape** and **Krita**, and there are simple online tools such as **Canva** or **Photopea**. More and more editors also offer **AI tools** that remove backgrounds or generate parts of a picture.

### Raster vs. vector graphics

| | Raster (bitmap) graphics | Vector graphics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| made of | a grid of **pixels** | mathematical **shapes** – points, lines, curves |
| when enlarged | becomes blurred or **pixelated** | stays sharp at any size |
| used for | photos, realistic pictures | logos, icons, illustrations, fonts |
| formats | JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, TIFF | SVG, AI, EPS, PDF |
| programs | Photoshop, GIMP | Illustrator, Inkscape |

### Basic terms and operations

- **Resolution** – the number of pixels (e.g. 1920 × 1080) or the density for printing in **DPI**; 72–96 DPI is enough for screens, 300 DPI for print.
- **Crop** (cut off unwanted parts), **resize**, **rotate** and **flip**.
- **Brightness**, **contrast**, **saturation**, **colour balance** and **retouching** – removing spots, red eyes or unwanted objects (the clone tool, the healing brush).
- **Layers** – transparent "sheets" placed on top of each other, so that you can edit one part of the picture without changing the others; **masks** hide parts of a layer.
- **Selection tools** – the rectangle, lasso and magic wand.
- **Filters and effects** – blur, sharpen, black and white.
- **Colour models** – **RGB** (red, green, blue) for screens, **CMYK** for print.
- **File formats** – **JPG** for photos (small but **lossy** compression), **PNG** for graphics with **transparency** (lossless), **GIF** for simple animations, **SVG** for vector graphics on the web, and the program's own format (PSD, XCF) to keep the layers.

### Ethics

Edited photos can be misleading – think of retouched models in adverts or fake pictures created by AI. It is important to say when a picture has been changed, to respect **copyright** and licences such as **Creative Commons**, and not to use other people's photos without permission.

## Useful phrases

- *To make a heading, you should use a style rather than…*
- *First you select the text, then you…*
- *The main difference between raster and vector graphics is that…*
- *I'd save it as a PNG because…*
- *I think AI tools will change image editing because…*
