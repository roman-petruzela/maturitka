Maturita topic 4, general part, at Cambridge B2 First level. The specific part of this topic is [Prague](/aj/realie/prague/) and [Places of Interest in the Czech Republic](/aj/realie/places-of-interest-in-the-czech-republic/).

## What to talk about

- living in a community – your town, neighbours, volunteering
- social events – parties, weddings, festivals, concerts, sports events
- living in a city vs. in the country
- social problems – unemployment, poverty, homelessness, addiction, crime, discrimination
- how society and individuals can help

## Model answer

People are social beings, and most of us belong to several **communities** at once – our family, our class, our neighbourhood or a sports club. I live in a small town where people still know each other, greet each other in the street and help each other when needed. In big cities life is more **anonymous**, but there are many more opportunities – jobs, culture and entertainment.

**Social events** are an important part of life because they bring people together. Personally, I enjoy concerts and music festivals in summer, and in our town there is a traditional fair every September, where the whole town meets. Then there are family events such as weddings, christenings and birthday parties. I think the pandemic showed us how much we need such events – when they were cancelled, many people felt **isolated** and lonely.

Unfortunately, every society also has its **problems**. One of the most serious issues in the Czech Republic today is the **cost of housing** – young people can hardly afford to buy or even rent a flat in big cities. Other problems are **poverty** and **debt**, **homelessness**, **addiction** to alcohol, drugs or gambling, and **discrimination**, for example against the Roma minority or people with disabilities. Big cities also face **crime**, **overtourism** and traffic, which are typical problems of Prague.

I believe that solving these problems is not only the job of the government. Of course, the state has to provide a **social safety net**, education and healthcare, but individuals can help too. Many people **volunteer** – they help in charities, food banks or animal shelters, or they **donate** money. At our school we organise a charity sale every Christmas and send the money to a children's home. Small things like this can **make a difference**.

## Grammar worth showing

- **passive** to talk about problems in general: *A lot of money is spent on… Many events were cancelled.*
- **should / ought to / it's high time** for suggestions: *It's high time the government did something about housing.*
- **too / enough**: *Flats are too expensive. Young people don't earn enough.*

## Useful phrases

- *One of the most serious problems is…*
- *This problem is caused by… / It leads to…*
- *I'm convinced that the government should…*
- *On the other hand, it's up to each of us to…*
- *A good example of this is…*
