Maturita topic 5, general part, at Cambridge B2 First level. The specific part of this topic is [The USA – Geography and Places of Interest](/aj/realie/the-usa/).

## What to talk about

- a healthy lifestyle – diet, exercise, sleep, stress, bad habits
- common illnesses and injuries, visiting the doctor, the healthcare system
- personal hygiene and body care
- mental health
- unhealthy trends – fast food, sitting at screens, addictions

## Model answer

I think a **healthy lifestyle** is based on a few simple things: a **balanced diet**, regular **exercise**, enough **sleep** and avoiding **bad habits** like smoking or drinking too much alcohol. It sounds easy, but in practice it's quite difficult. As an IT student I spend hours sitting at a computer, so I try to **work out** at least twice a week and go cycling at weekends. I also try to eat more vegetables and fewer sweets, although I must admit I have a **weakness for** chocolate.

**Mental health** is just as important as physical health. Many young people suffer from **stress** and **anxiety**, especially before exams, and social media can make it worse because we constantly compare ourselves with others. What helps me is sport, spending time outside and switching off my phone in the evening. If someone has serious problems, it's important to **talk to someone** or to **seek professional help** – it isn't a sign of weakness.

When I'm ill, I usually get a cold or the flu, with a **sore throat**, a **runny nose** and a **temperature**. In that case I stay at home and take some medicine. If it doesn't get better, I make an appointment with my **GP**, who examines me and may **prescribe** antibiotics. In the Czech Republic most healthcare is covered by **health insurance**, so people don't have to pay for most treatment, which is a big difference from the USA, where healthcare can be very expensive.

**Body care** is a natural part of daily life – having a shower, brushing your teeth twice a day, looking after your skin and hair and visiting the dentist regularly. Today people, both women and men, spend a lot of money on **cosmetics** and some even undergo **cosmetic surgery**. I think it's fine to want to look good, but we shouldn't be obsessed with appearance.

To sum up, health is something we usually **take for granted** until we lose it. That's why I think it's worth making small changes now – they will **pay off** in the future.

## Grammar worth showing

- **should / had better / ought to** for advice: *You'd better see a doctor.*
- **zero and first conditional**: *If you don't sleep enough, you can't concentrate. If I have time, I'll go jogging.*
- **gerund as subject**: *Eating too much sugar is bad for you.*

## Useful phrases

- *I try to… / I make sure I…*
- *I must admit that I…*
- *It's a well-known fact that…*
- *The best way to stay healthy is…*
- *If I were you, I would…*
