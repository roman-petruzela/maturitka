Maturita topic 7, general part, at Cambridge B2 First level. The specific part of this topic is [Parts of a Computer and Their Use, Types of Computers](/aj/it-english/parts-of-a-computer-and-types-of-computers/).

## What to talk about

- types of shops and where you like shopping
- shopping online vs. in real shops
- consumerism, advertising, sales and Black Friday
- your style of clothes, fashion trends, brands
- fast fashion vs. sustainable shopping, second-hand clothes

## Model answer

I wouldn't call myself a **shopaholic** – for me shopping is more of a necessity than a hobby. We do our **weekly shopping** in a big supermarket, where you can get everything under one roof, but I prefer small local shops and markets for things like bread or vegetables. When I need clothes or electronics, I usually **shop online**. It's convenient, I can **compare prices** and read **reviews**, and if something doesn't **fit**, I can return it. On the other hand, I sometimes miss the chance to **try things on** and get advice from a **shop assistant**.

As for clothes, my style is **casual** and comfortable – jeans, T-shirts, hoodies and trainers. I don't follow fashion trends very closely, and I don't care much about **brands**, but I like clothes that **suit** me and that are good quality. For formal occasions, like my school-leaving ball, I have a suit, which I hardly ever wear.

Fashion is a huge industry and it changes very quickly. **Fast fashion** chains offer cheap clothes that follow the latest trends, so many people buy more than they need and throw clothes away after wearing them only a few times. This has a negative impact on the **environment** – producing clothes uses enormous amounts of water and energy – and on the people, often in Asia, who make them for very low wages. That's why **second-hand shops**, clothes-swapping events and apps for selling used clothes are becoming popular among young people. I think it's a good trend: you save money, and you can find something original.

Advertising plays a big role in what we buy. Shops use **special offers**, **discounts**, loyalty cards and big **sales** like Black Friday to make us spend money, and influencers on social media promote products all the time. I try to think twice before I buy something and ask myself whether I really need it.

## Grammar worth showing

- **too / enough**: *These jeans are too tight. They aren't big enough.*
- **passive**: *Most cheap clothes are made in Asia.*
- **I'd rather + infinitive**: *I'd rather buy fewer, better clothes.*

## Useful phrases

- *I'm not really into shopping, but…*
- *The thing that annoys me most about shopping is…*
- *It's definitely worth paying more for…*
- *I'd rather…, because…*
- *I've recently noticed that…*
