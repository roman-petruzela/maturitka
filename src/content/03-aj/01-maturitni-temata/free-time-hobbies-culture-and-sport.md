Maturita topic 8, general part, at Cambridge B2 First level. The specific part of this topic is [Famous Czech People](/aj/realie/famous-czech-people/).

## What to talk about

- how much free time you have and how you spend it
- your hobbies – active and passive, alone or with others
- culture – books, films, music, theatre, galleries
- sport – doing sport vs. watching it, popular sports in the Czech Republic and in English-speaking countries
- how free-time activities have changed (technology, gaming, social media)

## Model answer

In my final year at school I don't have as much **free time** as I'd like, but I try to **make the most of** what I have. My biggest **hobby** is computers – I like programming small games and I'm **into** video games, especially strategy games, which I play online with my friends. I know that sitting at a screen all day isn't healthy, so I also do sport. I **go cycling** and in winter I **go skiing** with my family in the Krkonoše mountains. Twice a week I play **floorball** for a local team, which is a very popular sport among young Czechs.

Sport is a big part of Czech life. The most popular **spectator sports** are **ice hockey** and **football** – when the national team plays at the Ice Hockey World Championship, the whole country watches. Many people also go hiking, running or swimming, and the Czech Republic has a dense network of marked hiking trails. In Britain, the typical sports are football, **rugby**, **cricket** and **tennis** – Wimbledon is the oldest tennis tournament in the world – while Americans love **American football**, **baseball** and **basketball**.

As for **culture**, I enjoy going to the cinema and to concerts. I like reading too, mostly fantasy and science fiction, although I read more on my e-reader than in paper books. I don't go to the theatre very often, but last year our class saw a play at the National Theatre in Prague and I was surprised how much I enjoyed it. I also listen to music all the time – on the bus, while studying and when I'm running.

I think free time has changed a lot because of technology. Many young people spend hours **scrolling** through social media or streaming series instead of meeting their friends. Technology isn't bad in itself, but it's important to find a **balance** between online and offline activities. In my opinion, the best way to **unwind** is to do something active with people you like.

## Grammar worth showing

- **go / play / do** with sports: *go swimming, play tennis, do karate.*
- **love / enjoy / can't stand + -ing**: *I can't stand watching sport on TV.*
- **present perfect** for experience: *I've never been to a rugby match.*

## Useful phrases

- *In my spare time I'm really into…*
- *I've been doing… for… years.*
- *What I like about it is that…*
- *It helps me to switch off / clear my head.*
- *If I had more free time, I would…*
