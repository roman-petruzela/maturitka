Maturita topic 16, general part, at Cambridge B2 First level. The specific part of this topic is the comparison of festive meals in [Festivals and Holidays in English-Speaking Countries](/aj/realie/festivals-and-holidays-in-english-speaking-countries/) and [Festivals and Holidays in the Czech Republic](/aj/realie/festivals-and-holidays-in-the-czech-republic/).

## What to talk about

- your eating habits – meals during the day, favourite food, cooking
- Czech cuisine vs. British and American food
- healthy eating, diets, vegetarianism, food allergies
- eating out – types of restaurants, ordering, tipping
- food trends – fast food, food delivery apps, food waste

## Model answer

My **eating habits** are probably quite typical for a student. On weekdays I don't have much time for **breakfast**, so I just have a coffee and a roll. At school I have **lunch** in the canteen, which in the Czech Republic is usually the main warm meal of the day – soup and a main course. In the evening we have **dinner** at home with the family. At weekends my mum or dad cooks something special, and I've recently started cooking too – I can make pasta, risotto and pancakes.

**Traditional Czech cuisine** is rich and quite heavy. Typical dishes are **roast pork with dumplings and sauerkraut**, *svíčková* – beef in a creamy vegetable sauce with dumplings – goulash and fried cheese. Czechs also love soups and sweet dishes such as fruit dumplings. And of course, Czech **beer** is famous all over the world. **British food** has a reputation for being bland, but there are some tasty dishes: the **full English breakfast** with eggs, bacon, sausages and beans, **fish and chips**, the Sunday roast and **afternoon tea** with scones. In the **USA** people eat a lot of fast food like hamburgers, hot dogs and pizza, but American cuisine is also very multicultural – Mexican, Italian and Asian food is everywhere.

More and more people today are interested in **healthy eating**. They try to eat more fruit and vegetables and less sugar and fat, and many young people become **vegetarians** or **vegans**, for health reasons or because of animal welfare and the environment. Some people have to follow special diets because of **food allergies** or **intolerances**, for example to gluten or lactose.

I enjoy **eating out**, especially with friends. We usually go to an Asian restaurant or to a pizzeria, or we get a burger. When you go to a restaurant, you **book a table**, order from the **menu**, and at the end you **ask for the bill**. In the Czech Republic people usually leave a **tip** of about ten per cent, while in the USA a tip of fifteen to twenty per cent is expected, because waiters' wages are low.

One problem I'm worried about is **food waste** – about a third of all food produced in the world is thrown away. I think we should buy only what we really need and **use up leftovers**.

## Grammar worth showing

- **countable vs. uncountable nouns**: *a lot of vegetables, much sugar, a few apples, a little salt.*
- **used to**: *I used to hate vegetables, but now I love them.*
- **passive for recipes and traditions**: *Svíčková is served with dumplings.*

## Useful phrases

- *I'm not a big fan of…, but I love…*
- *It tastes / smells…*
- *Could I have…, please? / I'll have the…*
- *I'd recommend trying…*
- *In my opinion, the healthiest option is…*
