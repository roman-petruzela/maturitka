Maturita topic 3, general part, at Cambridge B2 First level. The specific part of this topic is [Festivals and Holidays in the Czech Republic](/aj/realie/festivals-and-holidays-in-the-czech-republic/).

## What to talk about

- a typical weekday – morning, school, afternoon, evening
- weekends and holidays – how they differ from weekdays
- household chores and who does them at home
- time management, stress and keeping a balance
- how daily routines differ – students, working people, pensioners, other countries

## Model answer

On weekdays my routine is fairly fixed. My alarm **goes off** at half past six, but I usually press the snooze button once or twice before I finally **get up**. I have a quick shower, get dressed and have breakfast – usually just a coffee and a sandwich, because I'm never really hungry so early. I leave home at about half past seven and take the bus to school, which takes about twenty minutes. On the way I listen to music or check my messages.

Lessons start at eight and finish between two and three, depending on the day. We have a short break between lessons and a longer one for lunch, which I have in the school canteen. After school I usually go home, have a rest for a while and then **do my homework** or study for tests. Twice a week I go to the gym, and on Thursdays I have a part-time job in a computer shop, which gives me some pocket money and experience.

In the evening our family has dinner together, which is one of the few moments when we're all at home. Then I **help with the chores** – I load the dishwasher and **take out the rubbish** – and spend the rest of the evening on my computer, watching a series or talking to friends. I try to **go to bed** before midnight, but I must admit I often **stay up** later than I should.

Weekends are completely different. I **sleep in** till about ten, and I spend the time with my friends, go hiking or visit my grandparents. Sunday evening is usually the time when I realise I still have some homework to do.

Since this is my final year, I have to **manage my time** better than before. I've started to plan my week, and I try not to **leave everything to the last minute**. I think the secret of a good routine is balance – enough time for school, but also for sport, sleep and the people you care about.

## Grammar worth showing

- **present simple + adverbs of frequency** for routines: *I usually / hardly ever / once a week…*
- **present continuous** for temporary situations: *This year I'm getting up earlier because I'm preparing for the exam.*
- **be used to / get used to**: *I'm used to getting up early. I can't get used to the new timetable.*

## Useful phrases

- *On a typical weekday, I…*
- *As soon as I get home, I…*
- *It depends on the day, but usually…*
- *What I like least about my routine is…*
- *If I could change one thing about my day, it would be…*
