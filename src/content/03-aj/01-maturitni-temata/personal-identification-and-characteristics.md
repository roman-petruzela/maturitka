Maturita topic 2, general part, at Cambridge B2 First level. The specific part of this topic is [Famous People from English-Speaking Countries](/aj/realie/famous-people-from-english-speaking-countries/).

## What to talk about

- basic personal information – name, age, where you live, school, interests
- your appearance – height, build, hair, face, clothes
- your character – positive and negative qualities, strengths and weaknesses
- how other people see you; first impressions
- describing another person – a friend, a family member or a famous person

## Model answer

Let me introduce myself. My name is…, I'm eighteen and I live in… with my family. I'm in my final year at a secondary school, where I study information technology, and after the **school-leaving exam** I'd like to go to university.

As for my **appearance**, I'm **medium height** and **slim**, with short dark hair and brown eyes. I usually wear casual clothes – jeans, a hoodie and trainers – but I can dress up for special occasions. People often tell me that I **look like** my dad, and I think I've also **taken after** him in character.

How would I describe my **personality**? I think I'm **easy-going** and **reliable** – when I promise something, I do it. My friends say I'm a good listener and that I'm **patient**, which helps when I explain something about computers to my grandparents. I'm also quite **ambitious**: I want to do well in my exams and in my future job. On the other hand, I can be a bit **lazy** when it comes to things I don't enjoy, and I tend to **put things off** until the last minute. I'm also quite **shy** with people I don't know, so first impressions are not my strong point – I need some time to open up.

My best friend is completely different. She's **outgoing** and **talkative**, she makes friends wherever she goes and she's always **full of energy**. She can be a bit **bossy** sometimes, but she's also very **generous** and **loyal**. I think we get on so well precisely because we **complement each other**.

In my opinion, character is more important than appearance. Looks can change, but qualities like honesty, kindness and a sense of humour are what make people really attractive. Of course, first impressions matter, for example at a job interview, but people should always **get to know** someone before they **judge** them.

## Grammar worth showing

- **be like** vs. **look like**: *What is she like? – She's friendly. What does she look like? – She's tall.*
- **tend to / can be** to soften criticism: *I tend to put things off. He can be a bit stubborn.*
- **order of adjectives**: *long curly black hair*, *a small round face*.

## Useful phrases

- *I would describe myself as…*
- *People who know me well would say that…*
- *My biggest strength / weakness is…*
- *I'm the kind of person who…*
- *She's not exactly…, but…*
