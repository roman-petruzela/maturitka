Maturita topic 12, general part, at Cambridge B2 First level. The specific part of this topic is [London and Places of Interest in the UK](/aj/realie/london-and-places-of-interest-in-the-uk/).

## What to talk about

- types of holidays – beach, city break, active, camping, staying at home
- how Czechs and you usually spend holidays
- planning a holiday – package holiday vs. travelling independently
- entertainment – cinema, concerts, theatre, festivals, nightlife, theme parks
- a holiday or an event you remember

## Model answer

For most people, holidays are the best part of the year – a chance to **get away from** everyday life, relax and recharge their batteries. People spend them in very different ways. Some prefer a **beach holiday** by the sea, where they can **sunbathe** and swim; others go on a **city break** to see sights and museums, and many people enjoy **active holidays** – hiking in the mountains, cycling or **canoeing**.

Czechs are known as a nation of travellers. In summer many families drive to Croatia or Italy, or fly to Greece or Egypt on a **package holiday**, where flights, accommodation and meals are all included. But many people also spend their holidays at home – they go to their **cottage** in the country, go camping or canoe down the Vltava, which is a typical Czech summer experience.

My family usually spends a week by the sea in summer and a few days in the mountains in winter. This year, though, I want to try something different – I'd like to go to a **music festival** with my friends and then travel around Britain. I'd love to see London, Edinburgh and the Scottish Highlands. When we plan a trip ourselves, we compare prices online, book a cheap flight and accommodation in advance and plan what we want to see, but we always leave some time for **spontaneous** ideas.

As for **entertainment**, there's something for everyone. I like going to the cinema and to concerts, and in summer I enjoy open-air festivals, where you can listen to music for several days, camp and meet new people. Some people prefer the theatre, musicals or stand-up comedy, others go clubbing, and families with children like **theme parks** and zoos. More and more entertainment happens at home, though – people stream films, play video games or watch concerts online.

The holiday I remember best was a trip to Scotland two years ago. The weather was terrible most of the time, but the landscape was breathtaking and the people were incredibly friendly. It taught me that a holiday doesn't have to be perfect to be **unforgettable**.

## Grammar worth showing

- **going to vs. will vs. present continuous** for plans: *I'm going to visit London. We're flying on Monday.*
- **second conditional**: *If I could go anywhere, I'd go to Scotland.*
- **I wish / if only**: *I wish I had more time for travelling.*

## Useful phrases

- *My ideal holiday would be…*
- *I'm really looking forward to…*
- *The best thing about… was…*
- *It didn't turn out as I expected, but…*
- *I'd highly recommend…*
