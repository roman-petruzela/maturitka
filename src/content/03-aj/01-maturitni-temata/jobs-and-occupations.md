Maturita topic 10, general part, at Cambridge B2 First level. The specific part of this topic is [Jobs in IT and Job Interview](/aj/it-english/jobs-in-it-and-job-interview/).

## What to talk about

- your dream job and what you consider when choosing a job
- the jobs of your family members
- types of jobs – manual vs. office, employed vs. self-employed, full-time vs. part-time
- working conditions – salary, hours, benefits, remote work
- unemployment and the future of work (automation, AI)

## Model answer

Choosing a job is one of the most important decisions in life, because we spend a third of our lives at work. When I think about my future job, the most important things for me are that the work should be **interesting**, that I can **develop my skills**, and of course that it is **well paid**. **Job security** and a good **working atmosphere** matter too – I'd rather earn a little less in a team I like than be paid a lot in a place where everybody is stressed.

My **dream job** is to become a **software developer**, ideally in a company that makes games or apps. I like the fact that in IT you can work almost anywhere in the world and often **remotely**. My parents have quite different jobs: my mum is a nurse and works **shifts**, including nights and weekends, which is very tiring, but she finds her job meaningful. My dad is an engineer in a factory and has **regular working hours**.

There are many ways to divide jobs. **Blue-collar workers** do **manual work**, for example in factories or construction, while **white-collar workers** work in offices. Some people are **employed** by a company and get a monthly **salary** and **benefits** such as paid holiday, meal vouchers or a company phone; others are **self-employed** and run their own business, which gives them freedom but also more risk. Students often have **part-time jobs** or summer jobs – I worked as a **shop assistant** last summer, which taught me a lot about dealing with customers.

The world of work is changing fast. Since the pandemic, **working from home** has become normal in many professions. **Automation** and **artificial intelligence** are taking over routine tasks, so some jobs will disappear, but new ones will be created. I think the most important thing is to be **flexible** and to keep learning. People today often change jobs several times in their career, which was unusual for my grandparents' generation.

## Grammar worth showing

- **future forms**: *I'm going to study IT. I'll probably work abroad. By 30, I'll have worked in several companies.*
- **would rather / prefer**: *I'd rather work in a team than alone.*
- **make vs. do**: *make money, make a decision; do a job, do overtime.*

## Useful phrases

- *The most important thing for me is…*
- *I wouldn't like to work as… because…*
- *It must be very demanding / rewarding.*
- *In the future, I can imagine myself working as…*
- *There are pros and cons to…*
